🕰 When's that tune?

Two or three teams

Hear a song, place it on your timeline. First team to fill their timeline wins. Leave Team 3 empty for a two-team game.

How it works
  • The active team hears a song and drops it into a gap in their timeline.
  • Right → the card joins their timeline. Wrong → nobody gets it.
  • 🎟 Tokens: each team starts with 2. Spend one to skip a song and get a different one (it's still your turn), or to challenge another team's placement — a correct challenge steals the card.
  • On your turn, trade 🎟 3 for a free card: it goes straight into your timeline, no guessing — and you still play your song.
  • Name both the title and the artist out loud to earn a token back.

Songs play as official 30-second preview clips from Apple Music — every reveal links to the full track. All music belongs to its rights holders.
