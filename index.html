Name your teams and choose how many cards win. Leave Team 3 empty for a two-team game.

How to play

  1. 🎧

    Listen. A song plays, a 30-second preview.

  2. 📍

    Place it in the gap of your timeline where you think it belongs. Right: the card is yours. Wrong: it's discarded, unless a rival challenges and steals it.

  3. 🎟

    Tokens. Every team starts with 2. Spend 1 to skip a song or to challenge a rival's pick, or 3 to buy a card. Name the title and artist before the reveal: +1.

  4. 🏆

    First to 20 cards wins. First up: Team 1
